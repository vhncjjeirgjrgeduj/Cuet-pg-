Official National Testing Agency (NTA) Rules

CUET (PG) Political Science (HUQP18) — CBT Instructions

1. Exam Pattern: Each Mock Test consists of 75 compulsory Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) carrying a total of 300 Marks with a duration of 90 Minutes.

2. Marking Scheme: Every correct answer awards +4 Marks. Every incorrect answer deducts −1 Mark (negative marking). Unattempted or Marked-for-Review (without answer) questions carry 0 Marks.

3. 5-State Question Palette:

  • Gray (Not Visited): You have not visited the question yet.
  • Red (Not Answered): You visited the question but have not saved an option.
  • Green (Answered): You have selected and saved an option.
  • Purple (Marked for Review): You have flagged the question for review without saving an option (will NOT be evaluated).
  • Purple + Green Border (Answered & Marked for Review): You have selected an option AND flagged the question for review — this WILL be evaluated for marks as per NTA CBT rules.
